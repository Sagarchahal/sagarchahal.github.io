# AgentBiz AI — 90-second demo script

## Before recording
- Open the project in Chrome at a readable zoom.
- Use fictional details only.
- Close unrelated tabs and notifications.
- Keep the demo honest: this is a local prototype, not a live AI company.

## Shot list and narration

**0–10 sec — Problem and idea**
“Small local businesses often need a simple, clear web presence. AgentBiz AI is a prototype for a digital service studio that structures the request, prepares a draft, checks it, and logs the order.”

**10–25 sec — Service offer**
“Here are three example services. The business model starts with one fixed-scope landing-page offer for tutors, salons, repair shops and solo professionals. The pilot prices shown in the plan are hypotheses to validate, not current sales.”

**25–45 sec — Customer order flow**
“On Client checkout, I enter fictional demo details, select a service and describe the goal. The app creates a quote summary. This is stored in my browser only; it is not connected to a server.”

**45–55 sec — Checkout safety**
“I click Simulate test checkout. The app clearly labels the event SIMULATED ONLY — NOT PAID. No payment provider is contacted and no money moves.”

**55–75 sec — Agent workflow**
“I open Overview and run the sample workflow. Intake structures the brief, Creation builds a template draft, Quality checks required sections, and Business logs the demo state. These roles are simulated in JavaScript, not live autonomous AI agents.”

**75–85 sec — Deliverable**
“I review the generated draft and download it as a text file. Human review is required before anything is sent to a customer or published.”

**85–90 sec — Roadmap**
“The next milestone is a secure backend, real AI API integration, human approval, and verified payment-provider integration after customer validation.”

## Suggested screen capture sequence
1. Services view and business model panel.
2. Client checkout form with fictional details.
3. Order summary and simulated payment status.
4. Overview workflow running.
5. Completed draft and download button.
6. README feature-status table.

## Do not claim
- That real payments are accepted.
- That the project has earned revenue or has paying customers.
- That the four roles are live AI agents.
- That a generated draft is automatically safe or ready to publish.
